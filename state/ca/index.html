Best Credit Unions in California (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-11-23

California is home to 7 credit unions on our review list, led by SchoolsFirst Federal Credit Union. Below: the strongest local options, plus nationally available credit unions any California resident can join.

Credit unions based in California

1. SchoolsFirst Federal Credit Union

Tustin, CA · 1.6 million+ members · NCUA insured

SchoolsFirst is the country's largest education-sector credit union and one of the clearest examples of a niche charter done well. Its product design assumes you get paid ten months a year: summer saver accounts smooth income across the gap, and its planning tools are built for school-district pay calendars rather than retrofitted from generic bank products.

Who can join: Current and retired employees of California public and private schools, colleges, and education-related organizations, plus immediate family members of existing members.

2. First Tech Federal Credit Union

San Jose, CA · 1.9 million+ members · NCUA insured

Merger update: First Tech and Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU) legally merged on January 1, 2026 into one credit union, First Technology Federal Credit Union (NCUA charter #23521, per First Tech's merger notice). First Tech and DCU still operate as separate divisions with their own websites, accounts and rates during integration, so this review covers the First Tech division; see our DCU review for the other. Because both are now one insured institution, NCUA coverage of $250,000 per ownership category applies to your First Tech and DCU deposits combined.

Who can join: Employees and family of 1,700+ partner companies (Microsoft, Amazon, HP, Intel and many more), residents of Lane County, Oregon — or anyone in the US via membership in the Computer History Museum or the Financial Fitness Association.

3. Golden 1 Credit Union

Sacramento, CA · 1.2 million+ members · NCUA insured

Golden 1 is California's everyman credit union: no employer requirement, no association dues, just proof you live or work in the state. With more than a million members and branches from Redding to San Diego, it functions as a statewide community bank that happens to be member-owned.

Who can join: All Californians — anyone who lives or works in California can join, plus family members of existing members regardless of location.

Full review →

4. Patelco Credit Union

Dublin, CA · 550,000+ members · NCUA insured

Patelco, born inside the Pacific Telephone company, is one of Northern California's anchor credit unions and one of the easier ones to join: beyond its 16-county footprint, anyone can qualify through the Financial Fitness Association, with Patelco covering the first year's dues. Its regular savings now pays a flat minimal rate, so the deposit value is in certificates rather than the small-saver tiers it was once known for.

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, attends school or worships in Alameda, Contra Costa, El Dorado, Marin, Merced, Napa, Placer, Sacramento, San Francisco, San Joaquin, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, Sonoma, Stanislaus or Yolo counties, or in Bakersfield, Eureka, McKinleyville or Santa Cruz; students/alumni of UC Berkeley, SF State, Cal State East Bay; employees of sponsor companies; family/roommates of members; or anyone who joins the Financial Fitness Association.

5. San Diego County Credit Union (SDCCU)

San Diego, CA · 404,000+ members · NCUA insured

SDCCU's pitch is refreshingly simple: checking that is free, period — no direct deposit requirement, no minimum balance, no monthly fee with conditions in fine print. In an era when even credit unions attach strings to 'free,' that unconditional structure is worth naming. Pair it with access to roughly 30,000 fee-free ATMs and the everyday banking case is strong.

Who can join: Anyone who lives or works in Imperial, Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego, San Luis Obispo, Santa Barbara or Ventura counties, California; immediate family or household members of SDCCU members; or anyone who joins the Financial Fitness Association ($8).

6. Redwood Credit Union

Santa Rosa, CA · 430,000+ members · NCUA insured

Redwood CU earned statewide admiration for its wildfire relief lending after the 2017 North Bay fires, and it consistently ranks among the best-run credit unions in the country. For Sonoma, Marin, and nearby counties it's the community institution to beat.

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, or owns a business in the North Bay and greater San Francisco region's qualifying counties, plus family of members.

7. Wescom Financial (Wescom Central Credit Union)

Pasadena, CA · 270,000+ members · NCUA insured

Wescom is greater LA's community credit union and UCLA's official banking partner, with branches across the Southland. It's a fee-light alternative to the big California banks with a particular strength in home equity lending.

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, worships, or attends school in California; UCLA alumni anywhere; family of members; employees of corporate partners.

How to pick in California

Start with eligibility: a local credit union with branches near you wins if you value in-person service, while an open-membership credit union usually wins on rates. Either way, confirm the institution is NCUA-insured (every credit union we review is) and compare its savings APY and loan rates against at least one online bank before committing.
