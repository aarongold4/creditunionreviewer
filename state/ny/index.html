Best Credit Unions in New York (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-11-23

New York is home to 4 credit unions on our review list, led by FourLeaf Federal Credit Union (formerly Bethpage). Below: the strongest local options, plus nationally available credit unions any New York resident can join.

Credit unions based in New York

1. FourLeaf Federal Credit Union (formerly Bethpage)

Bethpage, NY · 540,000+ members · NCUA insured

FourLeaf Federal Credit Union — the former Bethpage FCU, renamed — is Long Island's biggest credit union and one of the few large ones with a completely open national charter: $5 opens a share account from any state, no donation or association required. That simplicity has made it a national favorite, though today the competitive deposit rates are in its promotional certificate terms; standard certificates and the money market pay far less.

Who can join: Open to everyone in the US — anyone can join by opening a $5 savings account. No donation, association, or geographic requirement.

2. United Nations Federal Credit Union (UNFCU)

Long Island City, NY · 270,000+ members · NCUA insured

UNFCU exists for the UN's global workforce — people whose financial lives span duty stations and currencies. For members it handles international realities (wires, expat credit, overseas service) that US banks fumble; for everyone else it's admire-from-afar.

Who can join: UN system employees, retirees, NGOs affiliated with the UN, and their families — a genuinely international field of membership.

Full review →

3. ESL Federal Credit Union

Rochester, NY · 480,000+ members · NCUA insured

ESL — George Eastman's creation for Kodak workers — is now greater Rochester's leading financial institution of any kind, bank or credit union. Its annual Owners' Dividend has returned well over a hundred million dollars to members in good years.

Who can join: Live in one of 21 New York counties (incl. Monroe, Erie, Onondaga, Ontario, Wayne) AND either have a family/household member at ESL, live/work/worship/attend school in the City of Rochester, work for an eligible company or group, or join the George Eastman Museum (ESL pays for that membership). Immediate family of members also qualify.

Full review →

4. Hudson Valley Credit Union

Poughkeepsie, NY · 400,000+ members · NCUA insured

HVCU is the Hudson Valley's financial anchor between the NYC metro and the Capital District, with certificate pricing that regularly beats downstate banks and free checking across its seven-county footprint.

Who can join: Anyone who lives in CT, MA, NJ, NY, PA or VT can join through the HVCU Charitable Foundation (HVCU covers the fee); also immediate family/household of members, HVCU employees, and affiliates of the Community Impact Fund or American Consumer Council.

How to pick in New York

Start with eligibility: a local credit union with branches near you wins if you value in-person service, while an open-membership credit union usually wins on rates. Either way, confirm the institution is NCUA-insured (every credit union we review is) and compare its savings APY and loan rates against at least one online bank before committing.
