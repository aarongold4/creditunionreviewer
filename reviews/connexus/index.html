A one-time $5 association contribution opens membership to anyone in the country.

Who can join Connexus Credit Union

Residents of select Wisconsin, Minnesota, and Ohio communities, employees of partner groups — or anyone in the US via a one-time $5 contribution to the Connexus Association, handled during the online application.

Easiest path in: Anyone can join: the $5 Connexus Association contribution at signup opens membership nationwide. (cost: $5 association contribution + $5 share deposit)

Our take

The institution behind the rates is a Wausau, Wisconsin cooperative with a long specialty in RV and powersports lending and a deliberately digital national strategy. Service is adequate rather than luxurious, and the checking APY demands monthly activity. But for maximizing yield on serious cash inside NCUA insurance, Connexus and Lake Michigan CU are the two names to check first.

Pros

  • Anyone in the US can join for a one-time $5 association fee
  • Rewards checking pays a leading APY on up to $25,000 — the highest cap in the category
  • Certificates routinely rank among the best nationally
  • Strong specialty lending (RVs, boats, powersports)

Cons

  • Checking APY requires monthly activity requirements
  • Thin branch presence outside Wisconsin
  • Customer service is competent but stretched by national growth

Is your money safe at Connexus Credit Union?

Yes. Connexus Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
