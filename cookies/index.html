Cookie Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how CreditUnionReviewer.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let a site remember actions and preferences (like your cookie choice) and help us understand how the site is used. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

The cookies we use

Essential. A single small entry remembers whether you accepted or declined non-essential cookies, so we don't ask again on every page. This is required for the site to honor your choice and is always active.

Analytics (optional). If you accept, we use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure aggregate, de-identified usage — pages viewed, approximate region, device type, referral source — with IP anonymization enabled. These cookies do not load unless you accept.

Advertising (optional). Pages may display ads, and advertising partners may set cookies to limit repetition and measure performance. Where required, these load only with your consent. Affiliate links may set a cookie to attribute a referral if you open an account; this carries no extra cost to you.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, and opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com (Google Ads Settings), aboutads.info (Digital Advertising Alliance), and youronlinechoices.eu (EU). Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less tailored. See our Privacy Policy for how Google and other vendors use advertising cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest revision. Questions: use our contact form.

This is a general, good-faith cookie notice. If you operate in a strict-consent jurisdiction (EU/UK) or your ad network requires a certified consent management platform, pair this with an IAB-TCF-compliant CMP before relying on it for legal compliance.
