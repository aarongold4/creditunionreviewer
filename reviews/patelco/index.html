Patelco Credit Union Review (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-11-23

Patelco, born inside the Pacific Telephone company, is one of Northern California's anchor credit unions and one of the easier ones to join: beyond its 16-county footprint, anyone can qualify through the Financial Fitness Association, with Patelco covering the first year's dues. Its regular savings now pays a flat minimal rate, so the deposit value is in certificates rather than the small-saver tiers it was once known for.

Who can join Patelco Credit Union

Anyone who lives, works, attends school or worships in Alameda, Contra Costa, El Dorado, Marin, Merced, Napa, Placer, Sacramento, San Francisco, San Joaquin, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, Sonoma, Stanislaus or Yolo counties, or in Bakersfield, Eureka, McKinleyville or Santa Cruz; students/alumni of UC Berkeley, SF State, Cal State East Bay; employees of sponsor companies; family/roommates of members; or anyone who joins the Financial Fitness Association.

Easiest path in: Join the Financial Fitness Association (Patelco pays first-year dues) (cost: $0 to member: Patelco funds the $1 savings deposit and first-year FFA dues)

Our take

Full transparency requires noting the 2024 ransomware attack that took systems offline for roughly two weeks — among the most disruptive cyber incidents ever at a US credit union. Patelco provided credit monitoring, restored accounts, and rebuilt its security stack; members today bank at an institution that learned that lesson expensively. The fundamentals — free membership, fair loans, a full Bay Area lending menu — remain genuinely strong.

Pros

  • Anyone can join through the Financial Fitness Association, with Patelco covering the first year's dues and the $1 share
  • Broad Northern California eligibility across 16 counties
  • HELOC and mortgage menu built for Bay Area home values
  • A 2024 ransomware incident was handled with full member remediation — and prompted major security investment

Cons

  • Suffered a significant ransomware outage in 2024 (since remediated) — worth knowing the history
  • Regular savings pays a minimal APY at every balance
  • Branches are Northern California only

Is your money safe at Patelco Credit Union?

Yes. Patelco Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
