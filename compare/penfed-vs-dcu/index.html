The bottom line

PenFed is the better pick if certificates or a mortgage are on your radar; DCU shines for everyday banking and first-time savers. Both are genuinely open to everyone, so join whichever fits your near-term goal — or both.

PenFed Credit Union — pros

  • Anyone in the US can join — one of the largest truly open credit unions
  • Certificate and online savings rates that compete with internet banks
  • Full product shelf: mortgages, autos, cards, personal loans
  • Heritage of serving military members without requiring military ties

Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU) — pros

  • Open to anyone in the US via a small one-time donation
  • Pays a standout rate on the first $1,000 of savings
  • Auto loans available nationwide at sharp rates
  • Long track record as a digital-first institution

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone join DCU?

Yes. DCU offers membership to anyone in the US through participating organizations you can join at account opening, so no specific employer is required.

Which is better for a car loan?

Both are competitive; DCU is a popular auto lender and PenFed also prices well. Get pre-approved at both and compare the APR.
