Service Federal Credit Union Review & Profile (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

Service CU pairs deep military focus — including branches on bases in Germany — with one of the easiest open-membership paths anywhere: a free American Consumer Council membership. Its dividend-boosting direct-deposit perks reward primary banking.

Who can join Service Federal Credit Union

Military members and veterans, DoD personnel, residents of New Hampshire and nearby counties — or anyone in the US via free membership in the American Consumer Council.

Easiest path in: Anyone can join: pick the free American Consumer Council option in the application. (cost: $5 share deposit; ACC membership free)

Products

Military-focused CU with branches in Germany, 10% deposit-rate match perks, open to all via ACC.

Rates change frequently — verify current numbers at https://www.servicecu.org before opening an account.

Pros

  • Free nationwide membership path via ACC
  • Direct-deposit rate boosts and early pay

Cons

  • Branches limited to New England and military bases
  • Best perks require direct deposit relationship

Is your money safe at Service Federal Credit Union?

Yes. Service Federal Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
