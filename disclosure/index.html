CreditUnionReviewer.com is free to read and supported by advertising and affiliate relationships. This page explains, in plain terms, how the site may earn money and how that does — and does not — affect what you read.

How we may earn money

Advertising. We display ads served by third-party networks, including Google AdSense. Ad revenue depends on page views, not on what you buy, and advertisers have no say in our reviews, ratings, or rankings.

Affiliate and referral links. Some outbound links — the "Visit credit union," "Open account," and "Apply" buttons on our reviews and comparison pages — may be affiliate or referral links. If you open an account or take another qualifying action after clicking one, we may earn a commission or referral fee, at no additional cost to you. When a link is an affiliate link, it is marked with rel="sponsored". Where a credit union has no affiliate program, the same button simply points to its official membership page and earns us nothing.

What compensation never changes

Payment never buys a place on this site, a higher star rating, or a better ranking. Our five-factor scores (rates, fees, membership access, digital experience, and products) are editorial assessments reached the same way for every institution, and many credit unions we rate highly pay us nothing at all. Nothing on this site is individualized financial advice, and rates and terms change — always confirm current details with the credit union before you act. See our full disclaimer, privacy policy, and terms of use.
