Hope Credit Union Review & Profile (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-11-23

Hope is a community-development credit union channeling deposits into Black-majority and underbanked Deep South towns that banks abandoned. Anyone nationwide can join and hold insured savings that funds that mission — impact banking with NCUA protection.

Who can join Hope Credit Union

Individuals and businesses in the Deep South communities HOPE serves (Alabama, Arkansas, Georgia, Louisiana, Mississippi, Tennessee); membership is open to those who join Hope Enterprise Corporation (via the single application), and people outside the service area can join by opening a savings, certificate or money market account.

Easiest path in: Anyone can join: a one-time $10 membership fee (of which $5 is a contribution to Hope Enterprise Corporation) opens membership. (cost: $10 membership fee ($5 of it goes to Hope Enterprise Corporation) + initial deposit)

Products

Mission-driven CDFI credit union financing underserved Deep South communities; deposits open nationwide.

Rates change frequently — verify current numbers at https://www.hopecu.org before opening an account.

Pros

  • Deposits directly fund underserved communities
  • Open nationwide via small association fee

Cons

  • Rates are mission-fair, not market-leading
  • Thin branch and digital infrastructure

Is your money safe at Hope Credit Union?

Yes. Hope Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
