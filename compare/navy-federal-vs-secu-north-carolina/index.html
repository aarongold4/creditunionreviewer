The bottom line

These rarely actually compete for the same person — eligibility usually decides it. Both are model credit unions; if you qualify for either, it's an easy yes. If you qualify for neither, see our anyone-can-join list.

Navy Federal Credit Union — pros

  • Largest credit union in the country with 350+ branches, many on or near bases worldwide
  • Certificate specials and auto rates regularly among the best available anywhere
  • Very generous family eligibility — one veteran in the family tree can unlock membership for many relatives
  • 24/7 phone support staffed for military time zones

State Employees' Credit Union (SECU) — pros

  • Second-largest credit union in the US with a branch in every North Carolina county
  • A salary advance loan that is a genuine payday-loan alternative, not a fee trap
  • In-house mortgage servicing — your loan is never sold
  • Famously low and simple fee schedule

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone join SECU North Carolina?

No. SECU serves North Carolina state employees, certain public workers, and their family members. It is not open to the general public.

Which has lower fees?

SECU is renowned for an exceptionally low-fee, member-first approach. Navy Federal is also competitive, with benefits geared to the military community.
