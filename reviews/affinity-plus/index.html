Who can join Affinity Plus Federal Credit Union

Minnesota state employees, U of M community, residents of many Minnesota communities — or anyone via a one-time $25 donation to the Affinity Plus Foundation.

Easiest path in: Anyone can join: the $25 foundation donation option in the application covers all comers. (cost: $10 share + $25 one-time donation if joining via foundation)

Products

Member-obsessed Minnesota CU with superb service ratings and full digital banking.

Rates change frequently — verify current numbers at https://www.affinityplus.org before opening an account.

Pros

Is your money safe at Affinity Plus Federal Credit Union?

Yes. Affinity Plus Federal Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
