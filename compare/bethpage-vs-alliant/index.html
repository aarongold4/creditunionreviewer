The bottom line

Bethpage tends to win for CD shoppers and those who like having branches somewhere; Alliant wins for digital-only savers. Both accept members nationwide, so it comes down to product fit.

Bethpage Federal Credit Union — pros

  • Truly open nationwide membership with a simple $5 deposit
  • Certificates and money market rates that compete nationally
  • Strong home equity and mortgage operation
  • Long Island branch network for locals

Alliant Credit Union — pros

  • Effectively open to anyone in the US — Alliant even pays the qualifying association fee
  • High-yield savings and checking rates that compete with the best online banks
  • No overdraft fees and generous ATM rebates
  • Polished, genuinely good mobile app
