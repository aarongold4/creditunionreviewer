What this includes (and what it doesn't)

The result is principal and interest only — the part of your payment that pays down the loan. Your real monthly housing cost also includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, private mortgage insurance (PMI) if your down payment is under 20%, and any HOA dues. Those can add hundreds of dollars a month, so treat this figure as the loan portion, not your full payment.

Why the rate is worth shopping

Over a 30-year loan, a small rate difference compounds into tens of thousands of dollars. Credit unions often carry lower fees and competitive rates, and because they're member-owned, the savings flow back to you.
