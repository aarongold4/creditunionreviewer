Who can join Idaho Central Credit Union

Anyone who lives or works in Idaho, plus qualifying counties in Washington and Oregon, and family of current members.

Easiest path in: Idaho residency alone qualifies you. (cost: $25 minimum deposit)

Our take

Its expansion into eastern Washington and Oregon is steady rather than splashy, and its product economics follow the classic CU script — win on loan pricing and fees, stay merely respectable on deposit yield. For anyone living in its footprint, the practical advice is simple: get an ICCU quote before signing any Idaho mortgage or car loan.

Pros

  • Idaho's largest credit union and dominant local lender
  • Consistently strong mortgage and auto pricing in its markets
  • Branch coverage across Idaho plus growing WA/OR presence
  • Regularly ranked among best-performing CUs nationally

Cons

  • Eligibility limited to ID and nearby counties
  • $25 share requirement is higher than most peers
  • Deposit rates are regional-good, not national-good

Is your money safe at Idaho Central Credit Union?

Yes. Idaho Central Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
