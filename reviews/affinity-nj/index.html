Affinity Federal Credit Union Review & Profile (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

Affinity is New Jersey's biggest credit union and quietly one of the open-membership crowd — a small nonprofit donation at signup admits anyone. Garden State members get a dense branch network; everyone else gets competitive certificates online.

Who can join Affinity Federal Credit Union

Members of hundreds of New Jersey-area employers and associations — or anyone via the New Jersey Coalition for Financial Education (small donation) or Affinity Foundation membership during signup.

Easiest path in: Anyone can join: a small one-time donation to an affiliated nonprofit in the application qualifies you nationwide. (cost: $5 share + ~$5 one-time donation if joining via nonprofit)

Products

New Jersey's largest CU with open nonprofit join path, solid CDs, and full lending.

Rates change frequently — verify current numbers at https://www.affinityfcu.com before opening an account.

Pros

  • Small-donation path open to all
  • NJ's largest CU branch network

Cons

  • Best rates often require bundled relationships
  • Digital experience is mid-tier

Is your money safe at Affinity Federal Credit Union?

Yes. Affinity Federal Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
