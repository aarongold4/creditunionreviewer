The bottom line

If you qualify for Navy Federal, it's one of the best all-around credit unions in the country and worth joining. If you don't have a military connection, PenFed gives you most of the same big-CU advantages with no eligibility hurdle. For many people the choice is simply decided by whether they can get into Navy Federal at all.

Navy Federal Credit Union — pros

  • Largest credit union in the country with 350+ branches, many on or near bases worldwide
  • Certificate specials and auto rates regularly among the best available anywhere
  • Very generous family eligibility — one veteran in the family tree can unlock membership for many relatives
  • 24/7 phone support staffed for military time zones

PenFed Credit Union — pros

  • Anyone in the US can join — one of the largest truly open credit unions
  • Certificate and online savings rates that compete with internet banks
  • Full product shelf: mortgages, autos, cards, personal loans
  • Heritage of serving military members without requiring military ties

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone join Navy Federal?

No. Navy Federal requires a connection to the armed forces, DoD, or a family/household member who is already eligible. If you don't have that tie, PenFed is the closer open-membership alternative.

Is PenFed as good as Navy Federal?

For civilians, effectively yes — PenFed offers competitive rates and full-service products to anyone in the US. Navy Federal has a larger member-benefit ecosystem geared to the military community.
