How We Make Money

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

CreditUnionReviewer.com is reader-supported. Full transparency on how this site pays for itself:

Display advertising

Most pages carry display ads. Advertisers don't choose where they appear, can't buy placement in our rankings, and have no contact with our editorial process.

Affiliate partnerships

A small number of credit unions and online banks offer affiliate programs. When you open an account through one of those links, we may earn a commission — at no cost to you, and frequently with a better signup offer than walking in cold. Affiliate links are disclosed on every page that contains them.

What money never buys here

Rankings. Review scores. Pros-and-cons lists. The order of any table. Most credit unions we review — including some of our highest-rated — pay us nothing at all, which is the structural reality of this niche: credit unions are not-for-profits and most don't run affiliate programs. We'd rather recommend the right institution that pays us nothing than the wrong one that pays us well, because this site only works if you trust it.

What we are not

We are not financial advisors, and nothing here is individualized financial advice. We're a review publication. For decisions involving significant money, talk to a licensed professional.
