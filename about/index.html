About Credit Union Reviewer

Credit Union Reviewer exists because the credit union industry has a discovery problem: the institutions with the best rates in American consumer finance are also the hardest to figure out. Can you join? What's a field of membership? Is NCUA insurance real insurance? Most financial media skips credit unions entirely because — frankly — there's little affiliate money in covering them. We cover them anyway.

Who writes this site

NB

Nathan Blake, Editor

Nathan has spent a decade writing about consumer banking — fee schedules, rate sheets, deposit insurance rules, and the fine print most people reasonably never read. He's a credit union member himself, reviews every institution on this site against the same five-factor checklist, and maintains the verification dates on every data page. He is not a financial advisor, and this site doesn't give individualized financial advice.

What we believe

Three things. Credit unions are underrated and under-covered. Membership eligibility is far easier than most people assume — and telling people exactly how to get in is the single most useful thing a site like this can do. And honest coverage means saying when a bank is the better choice, which we do.
