1. Navy Federal Credit Union

Vienna, VA · 14 million+ members · NCUA insured

Navy Federal is the credit union most Americans have actually heard of, and for good reason: it is roughly three times the size of its nearest competitor and behaves like a full-service national bank that happens to return its profits to members. For military families it is close to a default choice — early pay deposit, base-adjacent branches, and lending products built around deployment realities like the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act.

Lending: Consistently low auto loan rates, VA home loans with no PMI, and credit cards with below-average APR ranges

Who can join: Active duty, retired, and veteran members of all branches of the armed forces, Department of Defense civilian employees and contractors, and immediate family and household members of any of the above. Family eligibility extends broadly — parents, grandparents, siblings, children, and grandchildren of someone eligible can all join.

2. State Employees' Credit Union (SECU)

Raleigh, NC · 2.8 million+ members · NCUA insured

SECU is what people imagine when they picture a credit union doing right by its members: a branch in all 100 North Carolina counties, a $1 checking account, and a salary advance product that exists specifically to keep state employees away from payday lenders. It is a deeply mission-driven institution that has resisted the temptation to chase growth outside its charter.

Lending: Low-cost mortgages serviced in-house, auto loans, and a salary advance loan designed to replace payday lending

Who can join: Employees of the state of North Carolina and public school systems, federal employees working in NC state agencies, NC National Guard, county employees in certain roles, and immediate family of current members.

3. SchoolsFirst Federal Credit Union

Santa Ana, CA · 1.5 million+ members · NCUA insured

SchoolsFirst is the country's largest education-sector credit union and one of the clearest examples of a niche charter done well. Its product design assumes you get paid ten months a year: summer saver accounts smooth income across the gap, and its planning tools are built for school-district pay calendars rather than retrofitted from generic bank products.

Lending: Auto loans, mortgages, and a paycheck-planning suite designed for 10-month educator pay cycles

Who can join: Current and retired employees of California public and private schools, colleges, and education-related organizations, plus immediate family members of existing members.

4. BECU (Boeing Employees' Credit Union)

Tukwila, WA · 1.5 million+ members · NCUA insured

BECU's name is its biggest marketing problem: most Washingtonians don't realize that 'Boeing Employees' Credit Union' has been open to every resident of the state for years. It is now the largest credit union in the Northwest and one of the largest in the country, with a charter that requires nothing more than living, working, or studying in Washington.

Lending: Competitive auto and mortgage rates, plus one of the lower credit card APR ranges among large issuers

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, worships, or attends school in Washington state or select Idaho and Oregon counties; Boeing employees anywhere; and members of partner associations.

5. Mountain America Credit Union

Sandy, UT · 1.3 million+ members · NCUA insured

Mountain America is the fastest-growing of the big Mountain West credit unions and the most commercially ambitious — it advertises heavily, opens branches aggressively, and runs a business-banking operation that rivals community banks. Members get a full-service institution that doesn't make you go elsewhere for any major product.

Lending: Full-spectrum lender: autos, RVs, mortgages, business loans, and credit cards

Who can join: People who live, work, worship, or attend school in qualifying areas of Utah, Idaho, Montana, Arizona, Nevada, and New Mexico; employees of select groups; and family of members. Membership in a partner association can also qualify residents of its footprint states.

6. VyStar Credit Union

Jacksonville, FL · 1 million+ members · NCUA insured

VyStar grew from Jacksonville's military base credit union into a million-member institution spanning 76 counties across two states. It pairs free everyday banking with one of the more serious business-lending operations among US credit unions — a genuine option for Southeast small businesses that feel unloved by regional banks.

Lending: Mortgages, autos, and one of the larger CU business-lending operations in the Southeast

Who can join: Anyone who lives or works in 49 Florida counties or 27 Georgia counties, plus family and household members of current members; past or present military served as its original base.

7. GreenState Credit Union

North Liberty, IA · 470,000+ members · NCUA insured

GreenState has spent the past decade transforming from the University of Iowa's credit union into the Midwest's most rate-aggressive depository — its CDs and money markets routinely top regional tables and occasionally national ones. It is also one of Iowa's largest mortgage originators, a rare scale achievement for a credit union.

Lending: Iowa's largest mortgage lender among CUs; strong autos and HELOCs

Who can join: Anyone who lives or works in Iowa, plus residents of dozens of qualifying counties in Illinois, Wisconsin, Nebraska, and South Dakota, and family of current members.

8. Members 1st Federal Credit Union

Mechanicsburg, PA · 500,000+ members · NCUA insured

Members 1st pairs a dense central-PA branch network with full-service lending, making it the default community option in the Harrisburg region.

Who can join: Live, work, worship, or attend school in central Pennsylvania counties, or family of a member.

9. Summit Credit Union

Madison, WI · 260,000+ members · NCUA insured

Summit is Madison's hometown credit union and one of Wisconsin's largest mortgage lenders, with an unusually deep financial-education program.

Who can join: Live or work in Wisconsin or select Minnesota counties.

Williston, VT · 170,000+ members · NCUA insured

NEFCU, now combined with VSECU, is Vermont's largest credit union and a leading mortgage lender in the state.

Frequently asked questions

Are credit union mortgage rates lower?

Credit unions often have lower fees and competitive rates, and many keep loans in-house rather than selling them. Always compare a Loan Estimate from a credit union against at least one other lender.

Do credit unions offer first-time homebuyer programs?

Many do, including low-down-payment and no-PMI options. These programs vary widely by institution, so ask specifically.

Can I get a mortgage if I just joined?

Usually yes — you typically just need to be a member when you apply. Joining can be as simple as opening a share account with a small deposit.
