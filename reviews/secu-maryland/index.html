Who can join SECU of Maryland

Maryland state employees and contractors, employees of partner organizations, alumni and students of many Maryland universities, and family of members.

Easiest path in: University of Maryland system ties (student, alum, staff) are the wide door. (cost: $10 minimum deposit)

Not eligible? See our list of credit unions anyone can join.

Maryland's largest state-chartered CU serving public employees and university communities.

Is your money safe at SECU of Maryland?

Yes. SECU of Maryland is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
