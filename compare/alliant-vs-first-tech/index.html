The bottom line

Alliant edges out for pure savers who want a top rate with minimal fuss; First Tech is the stronger all-rounder if you also want financing options. Both are easy to join nationwide, so eligibility won't decide it.

Alliant Credit Union — pros

  • Effectively open to anyone in the US — Alliant even pays the qualifying association fee
  • High-yield savings and checking rates that compete with the best online banks
  • No overdraft fees and generous ATM rebates
  • Polished, genuinely good mobile app

First Tech Federal Credit Union — pros

  • Open to anyone via a small one-time museum/association membership
  • Mortgage underwriting that actually understands RSUs and tech compensation
  • High-rate rewards checking on the first $1,000
  • Branches inside major tech campuses in the Northwest

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone join First Tech?

Yes. First Tech offers membership to anyone in the US, including through associations open to the public, so a tech-industry job isn't required.

Which is better for savings?

Alliant is more known for a consistently high everyday savings rate, but compare current APYs since both adjust over time.
