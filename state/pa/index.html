Best Credit Unions in Pennsylvania (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

Pennsylvania is home to 4 credit unions on our review list, led by PSECU. Below: the strongest local options, plus nationally available credit unions any Pennsylvania resident can join.

Credit unions based in Pennsylvania

1. PSECU

Harrisburg, PA · 500,000+ members · NCUA insured

PSECU runs a nearly branchless, digital-first model and passes the savings to members through ATM fee rebates and low loan rates.

Who can join: Pennsylvania state employees, students/alumni of many PA colleges, employees of hundreds of partner groups, and relatives of members.

Full review →

2. Members 1st Federal Credit Union

Mechanicsburg, PA · 500,000+ members · NCUA insured

Members 1st pairs a dense central-PA branch network with full-service lending, making it the default community option in the Harrisburg region.

Who can join: Live, work, worship, or attend school in central Pennsylvania counties, or family of a member.

Full review →

3. Citadel Credit Union

Exton, PA · 250,000+ members · NCUA insured

Citadel regularly posts some of the most aggressive CD and savings promo rates in the Philadelphia market.

Who can join: Live, work, worship, or attend school in the greater Philadelphia region (Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Lancaster, Montgomery, Philadelphia counties).

Full review →

4. American Heritage Credit Union

Philadelphia, PA · 250,000+ members · NCUA insured

American Heritage is one of the lesser-known anyone-can-join credit unions: a small one-time donation to its children's health foundation opens membership from anywhere in the US.

Who can join: Employees of 800+ workplace partners, residents of certain Philadelphia-area communities — or anyone who joins the Kids-N-Hope Foundation.

How to pick in Pennsylvania

Start with eligibility: a local credit union with branches near you wins if you value in-person service, while an open-membership credit union usually wins on rates. Either way, confirm the institution is NCUA-insured (every credit union we review is) and compare its savings APY and loan rates against at least one online bank before committing.
