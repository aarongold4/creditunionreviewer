Who can join SECU of Maryland

Maryland state employees (current, former, retired), employees of 1,950+ partner employers and state-funded organizations, students/alumni/staff of University System of Maryland and other listed schools, Anne Arundel County employees, and family/household members. Anyone else can qualify with a one-time donation of $10+ to the SECU MD Foundation.

Easiest path in: Anyone can qualify with a one-time $10+ donation to the SECU MD Foundation; University System of Maryland ties also qualify. (cost: $10 minimum share balance; $10+ one-time SECU MD Foundation donation if no other connection)

Is your money safe at SECU of Maryland?

Yes. SECU of Maryland is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
