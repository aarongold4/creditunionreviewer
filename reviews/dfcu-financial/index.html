DFCU Financial Review & Profile (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

DFCU Financial, born at Ford, pays members a special patronage dividend — a cash payout that has totaled over half a billion dollars since 2006, among the industry's most generous. Any Lower Peninsula Michigander can join.

Who can join DFCU Financial

Anyone who lives, works, or worships in Michigan's Lower Peninsula or select Florida counties, plus family of members.

Easiest path in: Lower Peninsula residency qualifies any Michigander. (cost: $5 minimum deposit)

Not eligible? See our list of credit unions anyone can join.

Products

Ford-born CU famous for one of the largest annual cash-back dividends in the industry.

Rates change frequently — verify current numbers at https://www.dfcufinancial.com before opening an account.

Pros

  • Long-running annual cash dividend to members
  • Open to all Lower Peninsula residents

Cons

  • Everyday rates are ordinary — the dividend is the deal
  • Detroit-area branch concentration

Is your money safe at DFCU Financial?

Yes. DFCU Financial is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
