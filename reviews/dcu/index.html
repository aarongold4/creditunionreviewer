Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU) Review (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

DCU was 'digital-first' before the term existed — chartered in 1979 to serve Digital Equipment Corporation and now a nationwide, million-member institution that anyone can join with a small one-time charity donation. Its calling card is the Primary Savings rate: an APY on the first $1,000 that typically multiples the national average, making it a favorite first account for kids, students, and emergency-fund starters.

Who can join Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU)

Employees and family of hundreds of partner companies, residents of select Massachusetts/New Hampshire communities — or anyone in the US via a one-time donation (around $10) to a partner nonprofit like Reach Out for Schools.

Easiest path in: Anyone can join: a ~$10 one-time donation to a participating nonprofit during the application qualifies you for life. (cost: $5 deposit + ~$10 one-time nonprofit donation if joining that way)

Our take

Beyond the headline perk, DCU is a workmanlike national lender — auto loans priced aggressively in all 50 states, fair mortgages, free checking. The technology is reliable but visibly older than Alliant's. The honest positioning: join for the first-$1,000 rate and the auto pricing, and judge the rest on your own needs.

Pros

  • Open to anyone in the US via a small one-time donation
  • Pays a standout rate on the first $1,000 of savings
  • Auto loans available nationwide at sharp rates
  • Long track record as a digital-first institution

Cons

  • The famous high APY applies only to the first $1,000
  • Branches exist only in Massachusetts and New Hampshire
  • App and web experience is dated compared with Alliant

Is your money safe at Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU)?

Yes. Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU) is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
