The bottom line

Geography and eligibility decide this one: BECU for the Pacific Northwest, SchoolsFirst for California educators. Both are top-tier within their fields of membership.

BECU (Boeing Employees' Credit Union) — pros

  • Open to all Washington residents, not just Boeing employees
  • Boosted first-dollar savings rate is great for people starting to save
  • Low, transparent fee schedule
  • Largest credit union in the Pacific Northwest

SchoolsFirst Federal Credit Union — pros

  • Largest education-focused credit union in the country
  • Products genuinely built around educator pay cycles (summer saver, paycheck planning)
  • Very low fee schedule and free checking
  • Consistently strong member-service ratings

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone join BECU?

Largely yes for Washington-state residents and some neighboring areas — BECU has a broad community charter, though it isn't a nationwide anyone-can-join credit union.

Who can join SchoolsFirst?

School employees in California and their family members. It's not open to the general public.
