1. Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU)

Marlborough, MA · 1 million+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

DCU was 'digital-first' before the term existed — chartered in 1979 to serve Digital Equipment Corporation and now a nationwide, million-member institution that anyone can join with a small one-time charity donation. Its calling card is the Primary Savings rate: an APY on the first $1,000 that typically multiples the national average, making it a favorite first account for kids, students, and emergency-fund starters.

Savings: Primary Savings pays a very high APY on the first $1,000 — a famous perk

Who can join: Employees and family of hundreds of partner companies, residents of select Massachusetts/New Hampshire communities — or anyone in the US via a one-time donation (around $10) to a partner nonprofit like Reach Out for Schools.

2. Alliant Credit Union

Chicago, IL · 900,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

Alliant is the credit union for people who would otherwise pick an online bank. It has no branches, a first-rate app, savings yields that track the national online-bank leaders, and a checking account that rebates ATM fees and refuses to charge overdrafts. The cooperative structure is almost invisible in daily use — in the best way.

Savings: High-yield savings consistently within striking distance of the best online banks

Who can join: Employees of partner organizations, residents near Chicago's O'Hare area — or, for everyone else in the US, free membership in Foster Care to Success, with Alliant paying the membership cost on your behalf.

3. PenFed Credit Union

McLean, VA · 2.9 million+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

PenFed is the easiest answer to the most common credit union question: 'can I even join one?' Since 2019 the answer here is yes, full stop — a $5 savings deposit makes anyone a member. That open door, combined with online-bank-grade deposit rates, makes PenFed the default national pick for people who don't have a local credit union connection.

Savings: Premium Online Savings with rates competitive with online banks

Who can join: Open to everyone. PenFed dropped its military-affiliation requirement in 2019 — any US citizen or permanent resident can join by opening a savings account with $5.

4. First Tech Federal Credit Union

Hillsboro, OR · 600,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

First Tech built its franchise inside the badge gates of Microsoft, Intel, and HP, and it remains the credit union that best understands technology compensation. If your income arrives as salary plus RSUs, First Tech's mortgage and personal-loan underwriting will read your pay stub correctly — something megabanks still fumble.

Savings: Standard and promotional savings tiers

Who can join: Employees and family of hundreds of technology companies (Microsoft, Amazon, HP, Intel and many more), residents of Lane County, Oregon — or anyone in the US via membership in the Computer History Museum or the Financial Fitness Association.

5. Connexus Credit Union

Wausau, WI · 480,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

Connexus is the rate-hunter's credit union. Its rewards checking pays a nationally leading APY on balances up to $25,000 — the most generous cap in the entire category — and its certificates show up on best-CD leaderboards so reliably that national personal-finance sites treat it as a fixture. A one-time $5 association contribution opens membership to anyone in the country.

Savings: Standard savings plus strong money markets

Who can join: Residents of select Wisconsin, Minnesota, and Ohio communities, employees of partner groups — or anyone in the US via a one-time $5 contribution to the Connexus Association, handled during the online application.

6. BECU (Boeing Employees' Credit Union)

Tukwila, WA · 1.5 million+ members · NCUA insured

BECU's name is its biggest marketing problem: most Washingtonians don't realize that 'Boeing Employees' Credit Union' has been open to every resident of the state for years. It is now the largest credit union in the Northwest and one of the largest in the country, with a charter that requires nothing more than living, working, or studying in Washington.

Savings: Member Advantage accounts pay an elevated rate on the first $500 — a clever incentive for small savers

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, worships, or attends school in Washington state or select Idaho and Oregon counties; Boeing employees anywhere; and members of partner associations.

7. Patelco Credit Union

Dublin, CA · 500,000+ members · NCUA insured

Patelco, born inside the Pacific Telephone company, is one of Northern California's anchor credit unions and the originator of a clever idea more institutions should copy: savings tiers that pay the highest rate on the first dollars rather than the last. A new saver's first $2,000 earns the top APY — exactly backwards from how banks reward balances, and exactly right for building a savings habit.

Savings: A 'rising rate' tiered savings that pays more on your first dollars — inverted tiers that favor small savers

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, or attends school in most Northern California counties (Bay Area and Sacramento regions), employees of partner companies, and family of members.

Frequently asked questions

Are credit union savings rates better than banks?

Often, though the top online banks are very competitive. The smart move is to compare a credit union's APY against the best online savings rate before deciding.

What's the difference between a share account and savings?

At a credit union, your basic savings account is called a 'share account,' and the small balance in it represents your ownership stake. Functionally it works like a bank savings account.

Is there a limit on savings withdrawals?

Federal limits on savings withdrawals were relaxed in 2020, but individual credit unions may set their own transfer limits. Check the account disclosure.
