Frequently asked questions

How often do credit union rates change?

Deposit rates (savings, CDs) can change monthly or faster, and loan rates move with the broader market. Always confirm the current rate on the credit union's site — the figures here are representative and dated.

Are credit union rates better than banks?

On average, credit unions beat traditional banks on savings, CDs, and loan rates because they return earnings to members. The best online banks are competitive on deposits, so compare both.

Why do some savings APYs only apply to small balances?

A few credit unions advertise a very high APY that only applies up to a cap (for example, the first $1,000). Above the cap, the rate drops to a standard level. Check the cap before assuming the headline rate applies to your whole balance.

Can I trust an advertised loan APR?

Advertised 'from' APRs assume excellent credit and often require autopay or other conditions. Your actual rate depends on your credit profile, loan term, and the vehicle or property.
