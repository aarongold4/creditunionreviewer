Patelco Credit Union Review (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-06-07

Patelco, born inside the Pacific Telephone company, is one of Northern California's anchor credit unions and the originator of a clever idea more institutions should copy: savings tiers that pay the highest rate on the first dollars rather than the last. A new saver's first $2,000 earns the top APY — exactly backwards from how banks reward balances, and exactly right for building a savings habit.

Who can join Patelco Credit Union

Anyone who lives, works, or attends school in most Northern California counties (Bay Area and Sacramento regions), employees of partner companies, and family of members.

Easiest path in: Bay Area and Sacramento residency covers the bulk of Northern California. (cost: $1 minimum deposit)

Our take

Full transparency requires noting the 2024 ransomware attack that took systems offline for roughly two weeks — among the most disruptive cyber incidents ever at a US credit union. Patelco provided credit monitoring, restored accounts, and rebuilt its security stack; members today bank at an institution that learned that lesson expensively. The fundamentals — cheap membership, fair loans, small-saver-friendly design — remain genuinely strong.

Pros

  • Inverted savings tiers pay the best rate on your first dollars
  • Broad Northern California eligibility with a $1 share
  • HELOC and mortgage menu built for Bay Area home values
  • A 2024 ransomware incident was handled with full member remediation — and prompted major security investment

Cons

  • Suffered a significant ransomware outage in 2024 (since remediated) — worth knowing the history
  • Best savings APY caps at a low balance
  • Eligibility is Northern California only

Is your money safe at Patelco Credit Union?

Yes. Patelco Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
