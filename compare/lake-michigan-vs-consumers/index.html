The bottom line

Both can out-earn a savings account if you meet the monthly criteria. Compare the current rate, balance caps, and exact requirements — they shift over time — and pick whichever pays more on your typical balance. Both join nationwide.

Lake Michigan Credit Union (LMCU) — pros

  • Anyone in the US can join with a $5 charity donation
  • Max Checking pays a leading rate on up to $15,000 — far above typical rewards-checking caps
  • One of the largest credit union mortgage operations in the country
  • Dual Michigan/Florida branch footprint

Consumers Credit Union (Illinois) — pros

  • Anyone in the US can join for a one-time $5 fee
  • Rewards Checking APY is often the highest checking rate in the country
  • Unlimited ATM fee reimbursement on qualifying tiers
  • No monthly maintenance fees

Frequently asked questions

Can I join Lake Michigan Credit Union from any state?

Yes — anyone in the US can join via a small one-time donation to its associated foundation, handled at account opening.

What's the catch with rewards checking?

You only earn the headline rate if you meet monthly conditions (debit transactions, direct deposit, e-statements) and only up to a balance cap. Above the cap, the rate drops sharply.
