1. Black Hills Federal Credit Union

Rapid City, SD · 110,000+ members · NCUA insured

Black Hills FCU is South Dakota's largest credit union, with statewide eligibility and a lending book that reflects the state's ranching and small-business economy.

Who can join: Live, work, worship, or attend school in South Dakota, or live/work/worship/attend school/volunteer in Yellowstone or Big Horn counties, Montana.

How to pick in South Dakota

Start with eligibility: a local credit union with branches near you wins if you value in-person service, while an open-membership credit union usually wins on rates. Either way, confirm the institution is NCUA-insured (every credit union we review is) and compare its savings APY and loan rates against at least one online bank before committing.
