1. Consumers Credit Union (Illinois)

Lake Forest, IL · 250,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

Consumers Credit Union of Illinois (not to be confused with the unrelated Michigan CU of the same name) runs what is frequently the highest-yield checking account in the United States. The Rewards Checking APY on up to $10,000 — unlocked by direct deposit and card-spend requirements — has topped national checking leaderboards for years, and unlimited ATM rebates sweeten the qualifying tiers.

Checking: Rewards Checking with one of the highest APYs in the country on balances up to $10,000, tiered by activity

Who can join: Open to everyone in the US — pay a one-time $5 membership fee to the Consumers Cooperative Association (handled in the application) and you're in from any state.

2. Lake Michigan Credit Union (LMCU)

Grand Rapids, MI · 480,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

LMCU is home to what may be the single best rewards checking account in America: Max Checking, paying a top-tier APY on balances up to $15,000 — a cap five to ten times higher than the typical rewards-checking gimmick. Hit the monthly activity requirements (direct deposit, logins, ten debit swipes) and you're earning online-bank yields on real money in a checking account.

Checking: Max Checking pays a high APY on balances up to $15,000 — one of the largest rewards-checking caps anywhere

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, or worships in Michigan's Lower Peninsula or select Florida counties — or anyone in the US via a one-time $5 donation to the ALS Foundation during the application.

3. Redstone Federal Credit Union

Huntsville, AL · 800,000+ members · NCUA insured

Redstone grew up with Huntsville's rocket program — chartered in 1951 to serve Redstone Arsenal — and remains Alabama's largest credit union, deeply wired into the aerospace-and-defense economy of the Tennessee Valley. For the region's engineers, contractors, and military families it is the incumbent financial relationship, with branch density no bank matches locally.

Checking: Free checking plus rewards tiers

Who can join: People who live, work, worship, or attend school in qualifying Alabama and Tennessee counties, Redstone Arsenal and NASA personnel, employees of hundreds of partner groups, and family of members. Association membership can qualify others in the region.

4. Randolph-Brooks Federal Credit Union (RBFCU)

Live Oak, TX · 1.2 million+ members · NCUA insured

RBFCU is the strongest argument in Texas for ditching a megabank. Its free checking pays interest from the first dollar with no hoops, and its no-annual-fee credit card pays a flat 2% back — a combination that simply doesn't exist at Chase or Bank of America. Add auto loan pricing that consistently leads the Texas market and the everyday-banking case writes itself.

Checking: Free checking that actually pays interest on every dollar

Who can join: People who live, work, worship, attend school, or volunteer in qualifying Texas counties (covering San Antonio, Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth and beyond), employees of select groups, military connections, and family of members.

5. Wright-Patt Credit Union

Beavercreek, OH · 480,000+ members · NCUA insured

Wright-Patt is Ohio's largest credit union and one of the most quietly mission-faithful big CUs in the country, with savings-builder accounts and small-dollar loan programs explicitly designed to pull members away from payday lenders. Its annual member giveback — a profit-sharing style patronage dividend paid most years — is the cooperative model made visible.

Checking: Truly free checking with early payday

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, worships, or attends school in central or southwest Ohio (Dayton, Columbus, Cincinnati regions), Wright-Patterson AFB personnel, and family of members.

6. State Employees' Credit Union (SECU)

Raleigh, NC · 2.8 million+ members · NCUA insured

SECU is what people imagine when they picture a credit union doing right by its members: a branch in all 100 North Carolina counties, a $1 checking account, and a salary advance product that exists specifically to keep state employees away from payday lenders. It is a deeply mission-driven institution that has resisted the temptation to chase growth outside its charter.

Checking: Single no-frills checking account with a flat $1 monthly fee and unusually low overdraft costs

Who can join: Employees of the state of North Carolina and public school systems, federal employees working in NC state agencies, NC National Guard, county employees in certain roles, and immediate family of current members.

7. BECU (Boeing Employees' Credit Union)

Tukwila, WA · 1.5 million+ members · NCUA insured

BECU's name is its biggest marketing problem: most Washingtonians don't realize that 'Boeing Employees' Credit Union' has been open to every resident of the state for years. It is now the largest credit union in the Northwest and one of the largest in the country, with a charter that requires nothing more than living, working, or studying in Washington.

Checking: Free checking with no monthly fees and a first-year rate boost on initial balances

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, worships, or attends school in Washington state or select Idaho and Oregon counties; Boeing employees anywhere; and members of partner associations.

8. San Diego County Credit Union (SDCCU)

San Diego, CA · 430,000+ members · NCUA insured

SDCCU's pitch is refreshingly simple: checking that is free, period — no direct deposit requirement, no minimum balance, no monthly fee with conditions in fine print. In an era when even credit unions attach strings to 'free,' that unconditional structure is worth naming. Pair it with access to roughly 30,000 fee-free ATMs and the everyday banking case is strong.

Checking: Free checking with no minimums and no direct-deposit requirement — a genuinely free account

Who can join: Anyone who lives or works in San Diego, Riverside, or Orange County, California, plus family of members.

Frequently asked questions

What is rewards checking?

A checking account that pays a high APY (sometimes 3–5%+) on balances up to a cap, if you meet monthly requirements like a set number of debit transactions and e-statements. Great if you can hit the hoops reliably.

Do credit unions really offer free checking?

Many do — with no monthly fee and no minimum balance — which is increasingly rare at large banks. Watch for overdraft and out-of-network ATM fees, which can still apply.

Can I use ATMs nationwide with a credit union?

Usually yes. Most credit unions belong to surcharge-free ATM networks (like CO-OP) with tens of thousands of machines, and many rebate out-of-network ATM fees.
