1. Bethpage Federal Credit Union

Bethpage, NY · 470,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

Bethpage — in the middle of rebranding as FourLeaf Federal Credit Union — is Long Island's biggest credit union and one of the few large ones with a completely open national charter: $5 opens a share account from any state, no donation or association required. That simplicity has made it a fixture on national CD and money-market rate leaderboards.

Certificates: Certificates that frequently appear on national best-of lists

Who can join: Open to everyone in the US — anyone can join by opening a $5 savings account. No donation, association, or geographic requirement.

2. Connexus Credit Union

Wausau, WI · 480,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

Connexus is the rate-hunter's credit union. Its rewards checking pays a nationally leading APY on balances up to $25,000 — the most generous cap in the entire category — and its certificates show up on best-CD leaderboards so reliably that national personal-finance sites treat it as a fixture. A one-time $5 association contribution opens membership to anyone in the country.

Certificates: Certificates that habitually rank on national best-CD lists

Who can join: Residents of select Wisconsin, Minnesota, and Ohio communities, employees of partner groups — or anyone in the US via a one-time $5 contribution to the Connexus Association, handled during the online application.

3. America First Credit Union

Riverdale, UT · 1.4 million+ members · NCUA insured

America First is the Mountain West's dominant credit union, with a footprint spanning six states and a $1 membership share that makes it one of the cheapest financial institutions in America to join. In Utah it is functionally a default banking choice, with branch density rivaling any bank.

Certificates: Flexible certificates including bump-rate and add-on 'ladder' options

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, worships, attends school, or volunteers in qualifying areas of Utah, Nevada, Arizona, Idaho, Oregon, and New Mexico, plus relatives of current members.

4. Alliant Credit Union

Chicago, IL · 900,000+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

Alliant is the credit union for people who would otherwise pick an online bank. It has no branches, a first-rate app, savings yields that track the national online-bank leaders, and a checking account that rebates ATM fees and refuses to charge overdrafts. The cooperative structure is almost invisible in daily use — in the best way.

Certificates: Certificates from 3 months to 5 years with low $1,000 minimums

Who can join: Employees of partner organizations, residents near Chicago's O'Hare area — or, for everyone else in the US, free membership in Foster Care to Success, with Alliant paying the membership cost on your behalf.

5. Navy Federal Credit Union

Vienna, VA · 14 million+ members · NCUA insured

Navy Federal is the credit union most Americans have actually heard of, and for good reason: it is roughly three times the size of its nearest competitor and behaves like a full-service national bank that happens to return its profits to members. For military families it is close to a default choice — early pay deposit, base-adjacent branches, and lending products built around deployment realities like the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act.

Certificates: Certificates from 3 months to 7 years, including frequent limited-time special rates and a low-minimum EasyStart certificate

Who can join: Active duty, retired, and veteran members of all branches of the armed forces, Department of Defense civilian employees and contractors, and immediate family and household members of any of the above. Family eligibility extends broadly — parents, grandparents, siblings, children, and grandchildren of someone eligible can all join.

Frequently asked questions

Do credit unions have better CD rates than banks?

On average, yes. Credit union share certificates have historically out-yielded comparable bank CDs because credit unions return earnings to members. The best online bank CDs are competitive, so compare both before locking in a term.

Are credit union CDs (share certificates) NCUA insured?

Yes. Share certificates are insured by the NCUA up to $250,000 per share owner, per credit union, per ownership category — the same protection as a bank CD under FDIC.

What's an early withdrawal penalty on a credit union CD?

Pulling money out before the term ends typically forfeits some interest — often a set number of months' dividends depending on the term. Always check the specific penalty before opening.
