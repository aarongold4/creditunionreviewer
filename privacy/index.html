Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CreditUnionReviewer.com ("we," "us") handles information when you visit this site.

Information we collect

Analytics data. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage: pages visited, approximate location, device type, and referral source. This data is collected via cookies and similar technologies and does not directly identify you.

Contact form submissions. If you contact us, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide. We use it only to respond to you.

Advertising data. Third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. These partners have their own privacy policies governing their data use.

What we don't collect

We never ask for or store financial account numbers, Social Security numbers, or credit information. Any site claiming to be us and requesting such information is fraudulent. When you click through to a credit union or bank, their privacy policy governs from that point.

Cookies and choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, and out of personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party links

This site links to credit unions, banks, and other external sites. We're not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest revision. Questions: use our contact form.
