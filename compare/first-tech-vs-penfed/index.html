The bottom line

PenFed tends to lead on certificate and mortgage rates; First Tech offers broader lending variety and a polished digital experience. Both are open to everyone, so let your specific product need break the tie.

First Tech Federal Credit Union — pros

  • Open to anyone via a small one-time museum/association membership
  • Mortgage underwriting that actually understands RSUs and tech compensation
  • High-rate rewards checking on the first $1,000
  • Branches inside major tech campuses in the Northwest

PenFed Credit Union — pros

  • Anyone in the US can join — one of the largest truly open credit unions
  • Certificate and online savings rates that compete with internet banks
  • Full product shelf: mortgages, autos, cards, personal loans
  • Heritage of serving military members without requiring military ties

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a tech job to join First Tech?

No. Despite the name, First Tech is open to anyone in the US through qualifying associations you can join at signup.

Which is better for a mortgage?

PenFed is frequently competitive on mortgage rates; First Tech also lends broadly. Compare Loan Estimates from both.
