How this finder works

This tool matches you on two things: credit unions that accept anyone in the US (via a small donation or free association membership), and credit unions headquartered in or serving your state. It can't see every employer or community charter in the country, so treat it as a strong starting point — always confirm eligibility on the credit union's own "who can join" page. Family ties matter too: if a relative already belongs to a credit union, you probably qualify there regardless of what this tool shows.
