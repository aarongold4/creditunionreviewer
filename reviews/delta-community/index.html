Delta Community Credit Union Review (2026)

By Nathan Blake · Last verified 2026-11-23

Delta Community outgrew its airline roots to become Georgia's largest credit union, and it carries one distinctly old-school virtue: its plain savings account pays one flat rate on every dollar, with no promotional tiers, caps or activity hoops. The rate itself is modest, but in a market full of asterisks that simplicity stands out.

Who can join Delta Community Credit Union

Employees of Delta Air Lines and a long list of partner companies, schools and associations; anyone who lives or works in 20 qualifying metro Atlanta-area counties; and relatives of current members.

Easiest path in: Metro Atlanta residency qualifies most Georgians who want in. (cost: $5 minimum deposit)

Our take

Combined with unconditional free checking and credit cards whose APRs start well below national norms, it makes a strong primary-bank case for anyone in metro Atlanta. Delta Air Lines employees worldwide remain the cultural core, and the airline connection still shows in 24/7 service coverage. Outside Atlanta and the partner-company list, though, there's no way in.

Pros

  • Georgia's largest credit union with broad metro Atlanta eligibility
  • Flat-rate basic savings with no caps or hoops
  • Low-APR credit cards
  • Free checking with no conditions

Cons

  • Eligibility limited to Atlanta metro + partner employers
  • Branches concentrated in Georgia with a few outliers
  • Certificate rates are mid-pack

Is your money safe at Delta Community Credit Union?

Yes. Delta Community Credit Union is federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA). Your deposits are protected up to $250,000 per share owner, per insured credit union, for each account ownership category — the same dollar protection FDIC insurance gives bank customers.
