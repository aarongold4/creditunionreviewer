The bottom line

Both are excellent and both are open to everyone, so you can't go wrong. PenFed leans full-service and rate-competitive across CDs and mortgages; Alliant is the slicker digital-only choice for savers. Many people who chase rates simply join both.

PenFed Credit Union — pros

  • Anyone in the US can join — one of the largest truly open credit unions
  • Certificate and online savings rates that compete with internet banks
  • Full product shelf: mortgages, autos, cards, personal loans
  • Heritage of serving military members without requiring military ties

Alliant Credit Union — pros

  • Effectively open to anyone in the US — Alliant even pays the qualifying association fee
  • High-yield savings and checking rates that compete with the best online banks
  • No overdraft fees and generous ATM rebates
  • Polished, genuinely good mobile app

Frequently asked questions

Is Alliant or PenFed easier to join?

Both are easy and open to anyone in the US. Alliant offers a simple path through a one-time $5 donation to its associated charity; PenFed lets you join by opening a savings account. Neither requires an employer or location tie.

Which has better rates, PenFed or Alliant?

It varies by product and changes often. Alliant is known for a strong everyday savings rate; PenFed frequently leads on certificates and mortgages. Compare current rates before deciding.
