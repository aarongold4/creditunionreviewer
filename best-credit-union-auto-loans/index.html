1. Security Service Federal Credit Union

San Antonio, TX · 800,000+ members · NCUA insured

Security Service is, above all, an auto lender wearing a credit union charter. Born at Kelly Air Force Base, it now finances cars at scale across Texas, Colorado, and Utah, often directly through dealerships — there's a fair chance a Texan reading this has been offered SSFCU financing at a dealer without realizing it. Its dealer-channel pricing is consistently among the region's sharpest.

Lending: Auto lending is the franchise — SSFCU is one of the largest CU auto lenders in the country

Who can join: People who live, work, worship, attend school, or volunteer in qualifying areas of Texas, Colorado, and Utah; military connections; and family/household members of current members.

2. Idaho Central Credit Union

Chubbuck, ID · 640,000+ members · NCUA insured

Idaho Central is the rare credit union that utterly dominates its home state: the largest financial cooperative in Idaho, the leading local mortgage and auto lender, and a perennial entry on industry 'healthiest credit unions' lists. In Boise, Pocatello, or Idaho Falls, ICCU is the default rate to beat on any loan.

Lending: Dominant Idaho mortgage and auto lender with consistently sharp pricing

Who can join: Anyone who lives or works in Idaho, plus qualifying counties in Washington and Oregon, and family of current members.

3. Navy Federal Credit Union

Vienna, VA · 14 million+ members · NCUA insured

Navy Federal is the credit union most Americans have actually heard of, and for good reason: it is roughly three times the size of its nearest competitor and behaves like a full-service national bank that happens to return its profits to members. For military families it is close to a default choice — early pay deposit, base-adjacent branches, and lending products built around deployment realities like the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act.

Lending: Consistently low auto loan rates, VA home loans with no PMI, and credit cards with below-average APR ranges

Who can join: Active duty, retired, and veteran members of all branches of the armed forces, Department of Defense civilian employees and contractors, and immediate family and household members of any of the above. Family eligibility extends broadly — parents, grandparents, siblings, children, and grandchildren of someone eligible can all join.

4. Suncoast Credit Union

Tampa, FL · 1.2 million+ members · NCUA insured

Suncoast is Florida's hometown giant — the state's largest credit union, grown out of Tampa's teacher community into a 40-plus-county institution covering most of the peninsula. Its community identity is unusually concrete: every debit card swipe sends money to local education foundations, a program that has funneled tens of millions of dollars to Florida schools.

Lending: Low auto rates, mortgages, and notably cheap credit card APRs

Who can join: Anyone who lives, works, attends school, or worships in most of the Florida peninsula's counties (40+ counties on the Gulf side and central Florida), plus family of members and alumni of Florida College campuses in the area.

5. Redstone Federal Credit Union

Huntsville, AL · 800,000+ members · NCUA insured

Redstone grew up with Huntsville's rocket program — chartered in 1951 to serve Redstone Arsenal — and remains Alabama's largest credit union, deeply wired into the aerospace-and-defense economy of the Tennessee Valley. For the region's engineers, contractors, and military families it is the incumbent financial relationship, with branch density no bank matches locally.

Lending: Autos, mortgages, cards, and significant member business lending

Who can join: People who live, work, worship, or attend school in qualifying Alabama and Tennessee counties, Redstone Arsenal and NASA personnel, employees of hundreds of partner groups, and family of members. Association membership can qualify others in the region.

6. Digital Federal Credit Union (DCU)

Marlborough, MA · 1 million+ members · NCUA insured · Anyone can join

DCU was 'digital-first' before the term existed — chartered in 1979 to serve Digital Equipment Corporation and now a nationwide, million-member institution that anyone can join with a small one-time charity donation. Its calling card is the Primary Savings rate: an APY on the first $1,000 that typically multiples the national average, making it a favorite first account for kids, students, and emergency-fund starters.

Lending: Low auto rates nationwide, plus mortgages and personal loans

Who can join: Employees and family of hundreds of partner companies, residents of select Massachusetts/New Hampshire communities — or anyone in the US via a one-time donation (around $10) to a partner nonprofit like Reach Out for Schools.

7. Golden 1 Credit Union

Sacramento, CA · 1.1 million+ members · NCUA insured

Golden 1 is California's everyman credit union: no employer requirement, no association dues, just proof you live or work in the state. With more than a million members and branches from Redding to San Diego, it functions as a statewide community bank that happens to be member-owned.

Lending: Auto loans are the franchise product, with first-time buyer programs; also mortgages and HELOCs

Who can join: All Californians — anyone who lives or works in California can join, plus family members of existing members regardless of location.

8. Mountain America Credit Union

Sandy, UT · 1.3 million+ members · NCUA insured

Mountain America is the fastest-growing of the big Mountain West credit unions and the most commercially ambitious — it advertises heavily, opens branches aggressively, and runs a business-banking operation that rivals community banks. Members get a full-service institution that doesn't make you go elsewhere for any major product.

Lending: Full-spectrum lender: autos, RVs, mortgages, business loans, and credit cards

Who can join: People who live, work, worship, or attend school in qualifying areas of Utah, Idaho, Montana, Arizona, Nevada, and New Mexico; employees of select groups; and family of members. Membership in a partner association can also qualify residents of its footprint states.

Founders is a Carolinas mainstay known for very low loan rates and a no-frills, member-first culture.

Frequently asked questions

Why are credit union auto loan rates lower?

Credit unions are not-for-profit and return earnings to members as lower loan rates. Federal credit unions also face an 18% cap on most loan rates, which keeps pricing in check.

Can I get pre-approved before car shopping?

Most credit unions offer auto loan pre-approval, which lets you shop like a cash buyer and gives you a rate to beat at the dealer. It's one of the biggest advantages of financing through a credit union.

Should I refinance my auto loan with a credit union?

If your current rate is high — common with dealer financing — a credit union refinance can cut your rate and payment. Compare the new APR and any fees against your existing loan.
