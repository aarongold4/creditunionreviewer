Can anyone join a credit union?

Short answer: yes — if you pick the right one. Every credit union has a "field of membership," but 17 of the credit unions we review let anyone in the US join, usually through a one-time charity donation of $5–$25 or a free association membership. If you've ever assumed you weren't eligible, start here:
